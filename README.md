# Came Through Audio drop box

This is where to send Claude photos, badges, music, videos, icons and screenshots.
It is a separate, private shelf of the project: **nothing here goes to Lovable or the website** until Claude places it.

## Fastest way: open this link

**https://github.com/quionapeterson-cta/came-through-audio/upload/dropbox**

It opens the upload screen directly (log in to GitHub first if it asks). Drag your files onto the page, or tap
"choose your files", then scroll down and click the green **Commit changes** button. That's it; tell Claude what you
dropped.

## How to drop files (any browser, phone or computer)

1. Open this page: https://github.com/quionapeterson-cta/came-through-audio/tree/dropbox
2. Click **Add file** (top right) → **Upload files**.
3. Drag your files in (or click "choose your files").
4. Scroll down and click the green **Commit changes** button.
5. Tell Claude in chat: "I dropped files in the drop box" and what they're for.

You can make a folder name part of the upload by dragging a whole folder, but you don't have to; just say what each file is.

## Limits

- Up to **25 MB per file** and 100 files per upload through the website.
- A longer video that's over 25 MB: share a Google Drive link in chat instead.

## What happens next

Claude copies each file to where it belongs (badge art to `public/icons/…`, photos to `public/home/…`, icons to the
favicon spots), resizes or converts it if needed, and then clears it out of the drop box so the shelf stays tidy.
Private MP3s are used for testing only and never placed on the website unless you say so.
