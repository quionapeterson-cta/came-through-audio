## Brand Identity

- **Font:** Poppins
- **Tagline:** YOUR FIRST STOP BEFORE DISTRIBUTION — always uppercase, 2px letter spacing; never "1ST".
- **Voice:** lead with the tagline and "Came Through" (Came Through Audio Came Through for …). Crowns and "Don't let em knock you out cho'crown" (the founder's personal branding) are used subtly, only now and then. See BRANDING.md §4.

Hierarchy: **Black and White are the primary brand colors.** Cobalt Blue is the signature accent and ranks below them.

1. **Black `#0b0b0b`:** primary text, "Came Through" wordmark on light backgrounds, dark mode base.
2. **White `#FFFFFF`:** primary text on dark, "Came Through" wordmark on dark backgrounds, light mode base.
3. **Cobalt Blue `#004AAD`:** the signature accent for buttons, highlights, navigation accents and accent borders.

- **Light backgrounds:** white base with black text.
- **Dark backgrounds:** black or dark charcoal base with white text.
- **Primary Black is `#0b0b0b`, not `#000000`.** Never use pure `#000000` for backgrounds or large text blocks. Pure `#000000` is only allowed for 1px borders, or in print PDFs if required.

Contrast rules:
- **Light backgrounds:** dark logo. "Came Through" in Black `#0b0b0b`, "Audio" in Cobalt `#004AAD`.
- **Dark backgrounds:** light logo. "Came Through" in White.
  - Small and medium text: "Audio" in Electric Cobalt `#4D8CFF`.
  - Large display text: "Audio" in Cobalt with a thin white outline.

Gold `#FFD700`, Medallion Gold `#B98339` and Platinum `#E5E4E2` are only for badges and awards, never general site design. Exception: the My Catalog health meter is liquid Medallion Gold in light mode and liquid Platinum in dark mode. See BRANDING.md.

In code, use the semantic theme tokens (`foreground`, `background`, `primary`, `ink`, …) rather than raw hex values, so every control follows the global theme.
